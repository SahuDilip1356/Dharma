## Anti-slop / restraint guardrails (design-md)

- Hairline borders, **no drop shadows**.
- One voltage (green); navy as ink + the two dark bands; gold for a single
  emphasis word only.
- Tight-tracked Inter Display headlines; sentence case.
- Journey strip = subtle progress fill only. No rotating cards, no scroll-jack,
  no animated counters (founder correction #4).
- Every section defines mobile reflow (single column; snapshot card stacks under
  hero copy; journey becomes vertical).
