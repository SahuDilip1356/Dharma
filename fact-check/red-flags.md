# Red-Flag Catalog (Step 5)

- **A Emotional manipulation (A1–A7):** inflammatory language, fear, urgency
  ("share before deleted"), tribal framing, victimhood, moral panic, disgust.
- **B Source/attribution (B1–B8):** anonymous authorship, uncited claims, fake
  credentials, anonymous "insider", known disinfo outlet, imitation domain,
  circular sourcing, misattributed quote.
- **C Logical fallacies (C1–C12):** cherry-picking, false dichotomy, straw man,
  irrelevant authority, hasty generalization, post hoc, whataboutism, slippery
  slope, moving goalposts, burden-of-proof reversal, naturalistic fallacy,
  argument from incredulity.
- **D Temporal/contextual (D1–D7):** old content as new, true facts in
  misleading context, selective edit/crop, headline vs. body, translation
  manipulation, satire as real, study misrepresentation.
- **E Coordinated/structural (E1–E6):** astroturfing, clickbait, deepfake
  indicators, bot distribution, cross-platform coordination, SEO manipulation.
- **F Health-specific (F1–F7):** miracle cure, adverse-event-database misuse
  (reporting ≠ causation), anti-institutional framing, naturalistic health
  fallacy, anecdotal cures, retracted studies cited, pseudoscience terminology.

Record each: code, location (quote the passage), severity
(Minor / Moderate / Serious).
